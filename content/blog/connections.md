_A connection between best friends  
A connection between loved one  
A long-distanced connection  
A connection with stranger  
A connection to the past  
A lost connection  
A connection to the future  
A connection with this world  
and the connection with myself  
​_

Recently, one of my best friend reaches out to me more often than usual. She fells lonely. It's Christmas. My lovely young girl who shines like the Moon, she actually lives her best 18's dream in her 25. And I'm so glad that she made it. However, the loneliness clings on her still, like the Moon need the darkness of night to be the brightest. And I feel that. I don't know if I should feel proud of myself or feel miserable to myself when my Moon reaches me when she down. She sent me the last long message of her ex to read. I just read it. And she never has enough courage to do so. I cried. He loves my Moon as much as I love her. She knows that really well, that's why she never read the message. I feel her, and I also feel the continued love of the one who was left. I've been both. It's painful for both.  
Seem like 2023 has been a painful year so far.   
Still, I love this beautiful world.  
So, can't wait to see more, 2024.
