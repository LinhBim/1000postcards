Post-cards - my obsession  
  
Some sent  
Some lost  
Some missing  
Some delivering  
Some waiting to be written  
Some waiting to be found.   
  
Up until now, I have been collecting about 200 postcards. I've received several ones from friends, and I've sent several ones to them, too.   
  
I've always been very dedicatedly taking care of my postcards in all phases: collecting those that deserved, choosing the matching one that may please the receiver, writing with thoroughness, sending at the right moment. After all of that, so many precious postcards lost before landing on the right hand. However, none of them to whom I ever feel wasted.  
  
I strolled around Paris, along Seine river, where I could find many mini kiosks that sell lost postcards. Many words. Imagine the amount of time and love that people dedicated to write there. Then not sent. But here started a new journey to those postcards. They are now living creatures that carry stories of other people, while travelling the world to find a destinater.  
  
That's beautiful, that creates a different story, that's definitely not wasted.  
  
But still, I have one wasted postcard.   
  
One of my first postcards that I collected.  
  
At the time, I didn't have many choice to send a suitable one to my cousin. I've picked the most-likely-to-match postcard and started writing. It was not that I would need to write to him, but because I've written to his sister, who is also my cousin, so I feel weird not sending another one. I forced myself to finish that postcard, but because there were none to convey, I stuck there. I left that miserable postcard unfinished because every words I tried to come up with just sound unnatural. Then it's not sent.  
  
That miserable postcard is still here with me, unfinished, unusable. I did not lose it in space, but I lost it in time. That's my lost postcard, the real lost.
