<p><img src="/images/postcards/301_orig.jpg" alt="Image"></p><p>It&#39;s been a while since I last bought a cute postcard. Sometimes, life hits you hard that you might forget what can make you happy and what embraces your soul. I got lost in the deep dark forest in my mind for about half year. Maybe that kept me away from buying cute postcard and update things in this little corner. Until when I got some backup from friends that I built up our friendships for long before, I finally managed to move my ass and back to the track to get out of the forest.</p><p>I got an excellent interview with an architecture agency that day. At some point I find that moment in that cozy room blend so well with my reality outside of the forest, with Notre Dame in front of the window and kind-hearted people surrounded.</p><p>Phillippe said  &quot;à bientôt&quot;</p><p>and Louis smiling</p><p>That reminded me of the shortest formal mail I&#39;ve ever received:</p><p>&quot;Bonjour,</p><p>Oui, tu peux venir.</p><p>A bientôt&quot;</p><p>I hope it&#39;s not a false hope that I&#39;ll have a good start soon.</p><p>Ah, and just after that, I saw this little cute cat in the postcard, as if it&#39;s always been there, without me noticing it.</p><p>I&#39;m glad that I see you my cute postcard.</p><p>Paris | 12.03.2025</p>
